Written Work

One of my first writing assignments for the University of Massachusetts Press was drafting interview questions for our newest poets. At the time, I could barely believe I had the opportunity to work with such accomplished authors. Since then, I’ve written press releases, blog posts, book copy, and of course, more interview questions for many more of our press’s talented writers. I remain so grateful that I’ve not only been able to promote such lovely people, but see my own writing grace their books. Check out some of my work attached to the links below.
